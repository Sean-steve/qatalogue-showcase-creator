04

Boiler Maintenance & Servicing

Keep your boiler operating safely and efficiently

Through qualified technical partners, Qatalogue can coordinate boiler servicing and maintenance solutions.

All statutory examinations and certifications should be undertaken by appropriately approved persons / competent service providers. Kenyan law requires thorough examination of steam boilers at least every 12 months, and DOSHS maintains a system for plant inspection and approved persons.

Coverage can include

  • Preventive maintenance
  • Boiler inspection support
  • Burner and combustion-system servicing
  • Pumps and motors
  • Valves and fittings
  • Pressure gauges
  • Safety systems
  • Water-level controls
  • Boiler cleaning
  • Troubleshooting
  • Repairs and component replacement

07

Boiler Water Treatment

Good water management protects your boiler

Poor-quality boiler water can contribute to scaling, corrosion, reduced heat transfer and equipment problems.

The specific treatment programme should be determined from the customer's water analysis and boiler operating conditions.

We can coordinate solutions covering

  • Boiler-water testing
  • Water-treatment systems
  • Chemical treatment
  • Water softening
  • Reverse osmosis where appropriate
  • Blowdown management
  • Feedwater treatment
  • Monitoring programmes
