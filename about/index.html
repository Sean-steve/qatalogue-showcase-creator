About Qatalogue

Built around one idea: waste is misplaced energy.

Qatalogue Limited is a Kenyan biomass and waste-to-energy company connecting residue producers with the industries that need dependable thermal fuel.

Our vision

A Kenya where industry runs on energy recovered from what was previously discarded.

Our mission

To supply reliable, responsibly sourced biomass fuels to industrial users while building the recovery networks that turn agricultural and forestry residues into commercial value for the people who produce them.

Our approach

From residue to running boiler.

01

Recover

We work with sawmills, farms, processors and forestry operations to collect residues that would otherwise be wasted.

02

Prepare

Feedstock is sorted, sized and processed into wood chips, industrial firewood or densified briquettes.

03

Supply

Fuel is delivered on programmes built around your consumption, boiler requirements and delivery schedule.

04

Improve

We stay involved — reviewing quality, consumption and cost with your technical and procurement teams.

What we do

Services across the biomass chain.

Industrial Biomass Fuel Supply

Regular supply of wood chips, industrial firewood and briquettes, structured around your operational requirements and delivery schedule.

Biomass Sourcing & Aggregation

A growing network connecting agricultural processors, sawmills, farmers and other biomass producers with industrial energy markets.

Fuel Supply Programmes

Programmes covering volume, quality, pricing, delivery and monitoring. You concentrate on manufacturing; we help keep your fuel supply moving.

Biomass Fuel Assessment

We work with your technical and procurement teams on current fuel, consumption, costs, boiler requirements and potential biomass alternatives.

Waste & Biomass Recovery Partnerships

Generating sawdust, coffee husks, macadamia shells, maize cobs or rice husks? Talk to us about recovery and commercial utilisation.

Future Industrial Energy Solutions

Longer term, partnerships around biomass boilers, industrial heat, steam generation, fuel conversion and energy efficiency.
